A Bay Area surveying practice, built on straightforward work.

Linework is a licensed land surveying practice based in the East Bay. We work with homeowners, architects, engineers, contractors, and developers on projects throughout the Bay Area — from residential boundary surveys to ALTA surveys for commercial transactions.

Every project is handled directly by the licensed surveyor. That means consistent communication, clear documentation, and someone who is accountable to the work from the first call to the final deliverable.
