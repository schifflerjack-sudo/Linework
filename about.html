About

Linework
Land Surveying

Precise work, clear communication, and a thorough knowledge of the Bay Area.

Linework is a licensed land surveying practice based in the East Bay. Our work covers the full range of land surveying services — boundary surveys, topographic surveys, construction staking, ALTA surveys, and permitting support — for clients throughout Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, San Mateo, San Francisco, and Santa Clara counties.

We work directly with property owners, architects, engineers, title companies, attorneys, contractors, and developers. Whatever the project, the licensed surveyor handling your work is the same person you speak with from your first inquiry through final delivery. That continuity matters — it means questions get answered accurately, problems get resolved quickly, and nothing falls through the cracks between staff.

The Bay Area has its own set of permitting requirements, recording standards, and agency expectations that vary county by county. We know those differences and factor them into every project from the start — which tends to keep things on schedule and avoids the delays that come from incomplete submittals or documentation prepared without local context in mind.
