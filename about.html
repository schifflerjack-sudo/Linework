About

Linework
Land Surveying

Our practice is built on precise work, clear communication, and a thorough knowledge of the Bay Area.

Linework is a licensed land surveying practice based in the East Bay. Our work covers the full range of land surveying services, including boundary surveys, topographic surveys, construction staking, and more, for clients throughout all Bay Area counties.

We work directly with property owners, architects, engineers, title companies, attorneys, contractors, and developers. The licensed surveyor on your project is the same person you will speak with throughout.

We stay with your project from initial fieldwork through final recordation, including any agency review and approval processes along the way.

About Jack

Hi! I am John Schiffler--everyone calls me Jack. I'm the Principal Land Surveyor of Linework. I live in Oakland and have been working in the East Bay for my entire surveying career. In a former life I worked as a lab technician after receiving my B.S. in Biological Sciences from UC Santa Barbara. A college roommate introduced me to this profession in 2019 and I have not looked back since. My approach to the work is highly collaborative; I have a wide network of trusted surveying colleagues and mentors throughout California. I love living in the Bay Area and take pride in working in this community.
