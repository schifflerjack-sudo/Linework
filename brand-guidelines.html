Wordmark — "Linework"

Cabinet Grotesk Bold. Never use a different weight or typeface for the wordmark. Letter-spacing: −0.02em.

Descriptor — "Land Surveying"

Satoshi Regular, set at approximately 58–65% of the wordmark size. Aligned left to the wordmark baseline.

Color combinations

Dark background: Ivory wordmark + Palm descriptor. Light background: Dark wordmark + Rust or Coral descriptor. Olive background: Ivory + Sand.

04 — Application

Color Usage

Primary Background

Dark (#1A1714) is used for nav backgrounds, footer, and CTA bands. Pairs with Ivory text and Palm or Sand accents.

Page Background

Cream (#F5EDE0) is the main page background for hero sections and content areas. Pairs with Dark text and Rust accents.

Section Contrast

Olive (#2A3B1C) and Forest (#2A5B1C) are used for high-contrast content blocks. Text in Ivory or Sand.

Accent / Call to Action

Rust (#C4562A) and Paprika (#EA5C1F) drive attention — buttons, eyebrows, links, and active states.

Warm Neutral

Sand (#DEC99A) works as a secondary text color on dark backgrounds and as a decorative accent in headlines.

Secondary Text

Mid (#7A6A58) is used for body copy, captions, and descriptive text on light backgrounds. Never on dark.
