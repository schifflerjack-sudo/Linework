Icon mark

The icon mark (survey instrument sight) is the standalone graphic element. It always appears as a single flat color — Ivory on dark or colored backgrounds, Dark Khaki or Dark on light backgrounds. Never use a fill different from the paired wordmark.

Wordmark — "Linework"

Cabinet Grotesk Black (900). No custom tracking or leading. On white or ivory backgrounds, use Dark Khaki (#413B25). On all other backgrounds, use Ivory (#F7F3E3).

Descriptor — "Land Surveying"

Satoshi Light (300). Approximately 58% of the wordmark size. The descriptor always matches the wordmark color — it is never independently colored.

Monocolor variants

On ivory backgrounds the wordmark may appear in a single mid-tone brand color: Crimson, Navy, Paprika, or Palm. Dark versions are not permitted. The descriptor always remains dark in monocolor variants.

04 — Application

Color Usage

Primary Background

Dark (#1A1714) is used for nav backgrounds, footer, and CTA bands. Pairs with Ivory text and Palm or Sand accents.

Page Background

Cream (#F5EDE0) is the main page background for hero sections and content areas. Pairs with Dark text and Coral or Paprika accents.

Section Contrast

Forest (#2A5B1C) is the primary high-contrast section background. Text in Ivory (#F7F3E3) or Sand.

Accent / Call to Action

Coral (#EF8354) and Paprika (#EA5C1F) drive attention — buttons, eyebrows, links, and active states.

Warm Neutral

Sand (#DEC99A) works as a secondary text color on dark backgrounds and as a decorative accent in headlines.

Secondary Text

Mid (#7A6A58) is used for body copy, captions, and descriptive text on light backgrounds. Never on dark.
